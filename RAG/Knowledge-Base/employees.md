# Aethon Dynamics — Employees

This document covers the leadership team and selected key employees at Aethon Dynamics.

## Leadership Team

### Dr. Maya Krishnan — Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
Maya co-founded Aethon Dynamics in 2016. Before Aethon, she spent eight years as a power
systems engineer at a regional transmission operator and holds a PhD in Electrical
Engineering from MIT. She owns the company's overall strategy and investor relations.
Maya is based in the Boston headquarters.

### Daniel Osei — Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
Daniel is the other co-founder and leads all of Engineering. He previously built
distributed systems at a cloud infrastructure company and is the principal architect
behind the VoltCore optimization engine. Daniel splits his time between Boston and the
Austin engineering office.

### Priya Venkatesh — VP of Engineering (VPE)
Priya joined in 2019 and manages the platform and infrastructure teams. She oversees
reliability, the data pipeline, and the GridSense backend. She reports to Daniel Osei.

### Marcus Bell — VP of Sales (Head of Go-to-Market)
Marcus joined in 2018 and leads the Go-to-Market division. He owns the enterprise sales
motion and the utility-partnerships program. Marcus is based in Denver and travels
frequently to customer sites.

### Sofia Reyes — VP of Product (CPO)
Sofia joined in 2020 from a grid-analytics startup that Aethon acquired. She owns the
product roadmap across all three product lines and runs the quarterly OKR process.

### Tom Whitaker — VP of Operations (COO)
Tom manages finance, people operations, and field deployment. He joined in 2017 and was
the company's first non-engineering hire.

## Selected Key Employees

- **Aisha Rahman** — Lead Data Scientist. Built the forecasting models that power
  VoltCore's battery dispatch predictions. Reports to Priya Venkatesh.
- **Leo Fontaine** — Principal Engineer, PulseAPI. Owns the market-data ingestion system
  and the trading API's low-latency path. Reports to Daniel Osei.
- **Grace Liang** — Director of Customer Success. Leads onboarding and renewals for
  enterprise accounts. Reports to Marcus Bell.
- **Ravi Mehta** — Senior Field Engineer. Heads on-site battery installations and is based
  out of the Denver operations team. Reports to Tom Whitaker.
