# Aethon Dynamics — Company Overview

## About

Aethon Dynamics is a clean-energy technology company that builds software and hardware
for electrical grid optimization and battery energy storage. Founded in 2016, the company
helps utilities, independent power producers, and large commercial sites monitor their
grid assets, optimize battery dispatch, and trade energy on wholesale markets.

The company is headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, with a secondary engineering
office in Austin, Texas, and a small field-operations team in Denver, Colorado.

## Mission

Aethon's stated mission is to "make the grid programmable" — to give operators the same
kind of real-time control and automation over physical energy assets that software teams
take for granted in the cloud.

## What We Do

Aethon operates across three product lines:

- **GridSense** — a real-time grid monitoring and analytics platform.
- **VoltCore** — a battery energy storage optimization engine.
- **PulseAPI** — a market-data and energy-trading API for wholesale electricity markets.

These products are sold both as standalone subscriptions and as a bundled "Aethon Grid
Suite" for enterprise customers.

## Company Facts

- Founded: 2016
- Headquarters: Boston, Massachusetts
- Additional offices: Austin, TX; Denver, CO
- Employees: 142 (as of Q4 2025)
- Funding: Series C, $180M total raised
- Lead investors: Cascade Climate Ventures, Northgate Capital
- Ownership: Privately held

## Structure

Aethon is organized into four divisions: Engineering, Product, Go-to-Market (Sales &
Marketing), and Operations. Each division is led by a VP who reports to the CEO. The
company runs on a quarterly OKR cycle and operates a hybrid work policy with three
in-office days per week for Boston and Austin staff.
