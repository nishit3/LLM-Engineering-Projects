# Aethon Dynamics — Products

Aethon sells three products. They can be bought individually or as the bundled
**Aethon Grid Suite**.

## GridSense — Grid Monitoring & Analytics

GridSense is a real-time monitoring platform for grid assets. It ingests telemetry from
substations, meters, and distributed energy resources, then surfaces anomalies, load
forecasts, and asset-health dashboards.

- **Primary users:** utility grid operators and commercial facility managers.
- **Key features:** real-time telemetry ingestion, anomaly detection, load forecasting,
  configurable alerting, historical analytics.
- **Pricing:** subscription based on number of monitored assets. Starts at $2,500/month
  for up to 500 assets; Enterprise tier is custom-priced.

## VoltCore — Battery Storage Optimization

VoltCore is the optimization engine that decides when to charge and discharge battery
storage systems to maximize value — arbitraging energy prices, providing grid services,
and avoiding demand charges. It is the company's flagship product and the source of most
of its revenue.

- **Primary users:** battery storage operators and independent power producers.
- **Key features:** price-aware dispatch optimization, demand-charge management, ancillary
  services bidding, degradation-aware scheduling.
- **Pricing:** priced per megawatt-hour of storage under management, plus a platform fee.
  Typical contracts run $80,000–$400,000 per year depending on fleet size.

## PulseAPI — Market Data & Trading API

PulseAPI delivers wholesale electricity market data and lets customers submit trades
programmatically across major US markets (PJM, CAISO, ERCOT, MISO).

- **Primary users:** energy traders, quant desks, and VoltCore itself (internal consumer).
- **Key features:** low-latency market-data feeds, historical price archives, programmatic
  bidding, settlement reconciliation.
- **Pricing:** tiered by API call volume and market coverage. Developer tier is $500/month;
  Professional and Enterprise tiers scale with usage.

## Aethon Grid Suite (Bundle)

The Grid Suite combines all three products with unified billing, single sign-on, and a
shared data layer so GridSense telemetry feeds directly into VoltCore optimization. The
bundle is sold only on annual enterprise contracts and represents the company's
highest-value deals.
