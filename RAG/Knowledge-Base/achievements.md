# Aethon Dynamics — Achievements

A record of Aethon's awards, milestones, patents, and notable press.

## Company Milestones

- **2016** — Aethon Dynamics founded in Boston by Maya Krishnan and Daniel Osei.
- **2018** — First commercial VoltCore deployment goes live; Marcus Bell joins to build
  the sales organization.
- **2019** — Crosses $1M ARR; opens the Austin engineering office.
- **2020** — Acquires a grid-analytics startup, bringing Sofia Reyes and the early
  GridSense team on board.
- **2022** — Signs first six-figure GridSense contract with Cascade Power & Light; closes
  Series B.
- **2023** — Passes 100 MWh of battery storage under management on VoltCore.
- **2024** — Closes $90M Series C; surpasses 1 GWh of storage under management.
- **2025** — Reaches $48.7M ARR and 142 employees; launches the Aethon Grid Suite bundle.

## Awards & Recognition

- **2023 GridEdge Innovation Award** — VoltCore recognized for its degradation-aware
  battery dispatch approach.
- **2024 CleanTech 50** — Aethon named one of the year's 50 most promising climate-tech
  companies.
- **2024 Best Places to Work (Boston Tech)** — recognized for its hybrid work culture and
  engineering practices.
- **2025 Energy Software Product of the Year** — awarded to the Aethon Grid Suite.

## Patents

- **US Patent (2022)** — "Degradation-aware scheduling for battery energy storage systems,"
  covering VoltCore's method of factoring battery wear into dispatch decisions.
- **US Patent (2024)** — "Low-latency market-data reconciliation for distributed energy
  trading," covering core PulseAPI technology.

## Notable Press

- Featured in a 2024 industry report on the top battery-optimization vendors, where
  VoltCore was cited for the strongest forecasting accuracy among the companies reviewed.
- Maya Krishnan named to an annual list of influential women in clean energy in 2025.

## Technical Achievements

- VoltCore's forecasting models, built by Lead Data Scientist Aisha Rahman, reduced
  dispatch forecasting error by roughly 22% over the prior generation.
- The PulseAPI low-latency path, owned by Principal Engineer Leo Fontaine, sustains
  sub-100-millisecond market-data delivery across all four supported markets.
