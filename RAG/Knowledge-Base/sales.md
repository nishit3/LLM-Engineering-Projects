# Aethon Dynamics — Sales

This document summarizes Aethon's revenue, customers, and sales pipeline. The Go-to-Market
division is led by VP of Sales Marcus Bell.

## Revenue by Year

- 2021: $6.2M ARR
- 2022: $11.8M ARR
- 2023: $19.5M ARR
- 2024: $31.0M ARR
- 2025: $48.7M ARR (preliminary)

VoltCore accounts for roughly 58% of annual recurring revenue, GridSense about 27%, and
PulseAPI the remaining 15%. The Aethon Grid Suite bundle drives the largest individual
contracts.

## Key Customers

- **Cascade Power & Light** — a regional utility running GridSense across 4,200 monitored
  assets. Aethon's first six-figure GridSense deal, signed in 2022.
- **Meridian Storage Partners** — an independent power producer managing 320 MWh of battery
  storage on VoltCore. The single largest VoltCore contract at roughly $390,000/year.
- **Brightline Renewables** — a Grid Suite customer using all three products across a
  portfolio of solar-plus-storage sites.
- **Halcyon Trading** — an energy trading desk and the largest PulseAPI consumer by call
  volume.

## Sales Motion

Aethon runs an enterprise, relationship-driven sales motion. The average enterprise sales
cycle is six to nine months and typically involves a paid pilot before a full annual
contract. VoltCore deals usually start with a single battery site and expand to the
customer's full fleet on renewal — net revenue retention across VoltCore accounts was
134% in 2025.

## Current Pipeline (Q4 2025)

- Total qualified pipeline: $22M in potential new ARR
- Largest open opportunity: a Grid Suite deal with a multi-state utility, estimated at
  $1.8M/year, in final procurement.
- New logos closed in 2025: 27
- Expansion deals closed in 2025: 41

## Regional Breakdown

The strongest market is the US Northeast (PJM territory), followed by Texas (ERCOT). The
California (CAISO) market is the fastest-growing region for VoltCore, driven by demand for
battery storage optimization.
